Privacy Policy

This policy describes how AIOVE handles account information, verification data, session data, and product usage information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect account details you provide, including email address, display name, login session data, and activity required to operate the service.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to authenticate accounts, secure access, deliver product features, detect abuse, and support account recovery.

3. Verification Data

Email verification records may include send time, expiration time, request origin, and attempt counts for security and fraud prevention.

4. Retention

We retain account and security records only for as long as needed to operate the service, support recovery, investigate abuse, and meet legal obligations.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical controls to protect account data, but no system can guarantee absolute security.

6. Contact

If you need account or privacy support, contact the product operator through the support channel provided for your deployment.
